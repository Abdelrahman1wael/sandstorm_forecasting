## 🌟 Executive Overview & Purpose of the Integration Hub

The **Integration Hub** serves as the **operational master blueprint** that translates theoretical research and standalone algorithms into a cohesive, production-grade scientific platform.

It synthesizes the project's **Three Scientific Pillars**:
1. **Pillar 1: Meteorological AI & Physics:** Main Line A (LightGBM/XGBoost/CatBoost) + Main Line B (Coupled AI-GAMFS + ST-GNN + PINN).
2. **Pillar 2: Spatial GIS & Cartography:** Ordinary Kriging exposure surfaces, Moran's $I$, LISA clusters, and Geographically Weighted Regression (GWR).
3. **Pillar 3: Socioeconomic Behavioral Science:** SPSS psychometric screening, AMOS Structural Equation Modeling (SEM), and Municipal Emergency Crisis Playbooks.
