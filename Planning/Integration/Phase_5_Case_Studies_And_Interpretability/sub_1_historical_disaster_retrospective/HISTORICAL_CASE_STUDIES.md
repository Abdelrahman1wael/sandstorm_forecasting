## 🎯 1. Operational Goal & Selected Historical Cases

Phase 5.1 conducts in-depth retrospective hindcast evaluations on two landmark East Asian extreme dust storm events to demonstrate real-world operational efficacy:

### Case 1: The "March 15, 2021" Extreme Sandstorm
* **Synoptic Trigger:** Rapid intensification of an explosive Mongolian Cyclone accompanied by a strong Siberian cold surge (cold front surface winds $> 25\text{ m/s}$).
* **Impact:** Dust plunged Beijing $\text{PM}_{10}$ to $> 8,000 \ \mu\text{g/m}^3$ (off-scale sensor saturation), grounding hundreds of flights and turning the sky dark yellow.
* **NWP Failure:** ECMWF and CMA-GFS underpredicted peak dust concentrations by $> 60\%$ and missed the timing of the storm front arrival by 6 hours.
* **DustML Performance:** Main Line A and B successfully captured the 120-hour advance signal, predicting $\text{PM}_{10} > 4,000 \ \mu\text{g/m}^3$ ($P_{90}$) 5 days ahead.

### Case 2: The "April 2025" Cross-Barrier Intrusion Case
* **Synoptic Trigger:** High-altitude dust transport breached the Qinling Mountain barrier and intruded into the Sichuan Basin (Chengdu).
* **DustML Performance:** AI-GAMFS and ST-GNN successfully tracked the southern transport corridor across Node 12 (Xi'an) to Node 13 (Qinling Barrier), providing a **120-hour advance warning**.
