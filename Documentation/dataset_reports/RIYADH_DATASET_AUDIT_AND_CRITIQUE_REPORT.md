## 1. Executive Summary: Real vs. Synthetic Status

The dataset in `data/raw/` is **a hybrid blend of real observational and reanalysis data, empirically calibrated fields, and synthetic stand-in files** created during pipeline development:

```
                            ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                            │            data/raw/ Modalities              │
                            └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                                   │
         ┌───────────────────┬─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┬──────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                     ▼                     ▼                  ▼
   [Ground ASOS]       [ECMWF ERA5]         [GIS Topography]       [Satellite & Solar]     [NWP Forecasts]
 100% Real Weather   100% Real Dynamics    100% Real SRTM DEM      Real MODIS/Sentinel    Mixed Components
  (PM10 is Derived)     & Soil Moisture     & Vector Corridors    (AERONET is Simulated)  (CAMS Grid Stand-in)
```

### High-Level Modality Scorecard
* **Ground Weather Stations (`data/raw/ground/`):** 🟡 **Real Telemetry + Derived Target.** All surface meteorological fields (wind, gusts, visibility, pressure, temperatures, WMO weather codes) are 100% real measurements from NOAA / IEM METAR airport networks. However, continuous $\text{PM}_{10}$ is an empirical calculation from visibility.
* **Atmospheric Reanalysis (`data/raw/era5/`):** 🟢 **100% Real Scientific Reanalysis.** ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis time-series (2020–2024 hourly) pulled via Open-Meteo Historical API.
* **GIS Topography & Geometry (`data/raw/gis/`):** 🟢 **100% Real Topography.** Digital Elevation Model (SRTM 90m) and surveyed geographic boundary polygons.
* **Satellite Remote Sensing (`data/raw/satellite/`):** 🟡 **Mostly Real + 1 Simulated File.** Satellite AOD/dust (MODIS/Sentinel assimilation) and NASA POWER solar irradiance are real. `aeronet_solar_village_riyadh_aod.csv` is **synthetic** (generated by a fallback simulation function).
* **NWP Forecasts (`data/raw/nwp/`):** 🔴 **Mixed (Real Operational Point Forecasts + Synthetic NetCDF Grid).** ECMWF IFS 10-day forecast and 5-day CAMS point forecasts are real. `cams_forecast_saudi_arabia.nc` is a **synthetic stand-in NetCDF**.

## 4. Scientific Diagnosis of Data Weaknesses & Artifacts

### 4.1 The "53.7 µg/m³" Ground PM10 Artifact

#### The Mathematical Root Cause
In `scripts/data_acquisition/download_all_riyadh_and_deserts.py` (lines 114–120):
```python
vis = np.clip(df["visibility_km"], 0.05, 30.0)
pm10_est = np.where(
    df["is_dust_event"],
    np.clip(1850.0 / (vis ** 1.08), 80.0, 4800.0),
    np.clip(380.0 / (vis ** 0.85), 25.0, 250.0)
)
```
1. **The 53.7 Constant:** Aviation METAR messages standardly report clean desert visibility as $10\,\text{km}$ (`9999` meters). In clear conditions (accounting for $84\%$ of records):
   $$\text{PM}_{10} = \frac{380}{10.0^{0.85}} = \frac{380}{7.079} = \mathbf{53.68 \approx 53.7\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3}$$
2. **Only 39 Unique Values:** Aviation weather human observers and transmissometers report visibility strictly in discrete standardized buckets ($10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2.5, 2, 1.5, 1, 0.8, 0.5, 0.25\,\text{km}$). Running 39 discrete inputs through a deterministic algebraic power-law formula produces exactly 39 discrete outputs.
3. **The 4800 Cap:** Represents `clip(..., 4800.0)`.

#### Scientific Verdict
Any regression model trained to predict `pm10_calibrated_ugm3` is **not learning aerosol physics; it is performing an inverse regression against visibility through a fixed non-linear power curve.** The true ground-truth signal is **Visibility + METAR present weather codes (`BLDU`, `DS`, `SS`, `HZ`)**.

### 4.2 The Synthetic AERONET Sun-Photometer Flaw

#### The Mathematical Root Cause
In `scripts/data_acquisition/download_aeronet_solar_village.py`:
When the automated HTTP query to NASA's server timed out or was rate-limited, the script triggered a fallback generator `generate_climatological_aeronet()`:
```python
dates = pd.date_range("2020-01-01 00:00:00", "2024-12-31 23:00:00", freq="3h", tz="UTC")
seasonal_cycle = 0.35 + 0.25 * np.sin(2 * np.pi * (doy - 80) / 365.25)
angstrom_440_870 = np.clip(0.85 - 0.45 * (aod_500nm / 1.5), 0.02, 1.25)
```

#### Scientific Verdict
* A CIMEL CE318 sun photometer measures **direct solar extinction**. It cannot operate at night (00:00, 03:00, 21:00 UTC).
* Real photometers have missing records due to cloud cover, calibration, and filter errors; a 5-year gap-free dataset is impossible.
* The narrow Ångström range ($0.34\text{--}0.82$) and near-zero correlation ($r = 0.05$) are direct consequences of the artificial sine wave.
* **Verdict:** Drop `aeronet_*` entirely from the feature matrix to avoid polluting model interpretations (e.g. SHAP values).

### 4.3 CAMS Weak Hourly Correlation & Temporal Latency

#### The Mathematical Root Cause
1. **Spatial Scale Mismatch:** Severe Arabian sandstorms (particularly spring *Haboobs*) are generated by convective cold-pool downdrafts on a scale of $1\text{--}10\,\text{km}$. CAMS operates at a coarse global resolution of $0.4^\circ \approx 40\,\text{km}$, smoothing out convective walls of dust.
2. **Phase / Arrival Latency:** A synoptic northwesterly *Shamal* front predicted by CAMS frequently arrives at Riyadh King Khalid Airport $3\text{--}6$ hours earlier or later than the numerical model timing. An uncorrected 4-hour time shift immediately drops the instantaneous Pearson correlation from $0.65$ down to $0.05$.
3. **The 70th Percentile Finding:** The fact that CAMS dust sits at the 70th percentile during 26 severe events proves that CAMS **does capture the regional atmospheric anomaly**, but misses the extreme localized peak.

#### Scientific Verdict
CAMS should not be evaluated as instantaneous ground truth. It must be ingested with a **rolling max window ($\pm 3\,\text{h}$)** to absorb frontal phase latency.

### 4.4 The Flawed "Number of Aligned Deserts" Feature

#### The Mathematical Root Cause
* At any given hour $t$, the regional wind vector over Riyadh has a **single directional azimuth** $\theta_{\text{wind}}(t)$ (e.g., $325^\circ$ during a Shamal or $145^\circ$ during Khasif).
* Riyadh's surrounding sand seas are in opposing geographic quadrants:
  - Nafud al-Thuwairat is **Northwest** ($325^\circ$)
  - Al-Thumamah & Ad-Dahna are **Northeast** ($45^\circ\text{--}60^\circ$)
  - Rub' al-Khali is **Southeast** ($145^\circ$)
  - Tuwayq Escarpment is **Southwest** ($235^\circ$)
* Sand cannot simultaneously blow into Riyadh from both the Northwest and the Southeast. Summing how many deserts have a positive cosine projection creates a noisy, degenerate integer feature that exhibits zero discriminative power ($2.22\%$ vs $2.26\%$).

#### Scientific Verdict
Replace the count with **Single Maximum Active Upwind Corridor Forcing**:
$$\text{Upwind\_Dust\_Forcing} = \max_{d \in \text{Deserts}} \left[ \max(0, \cos(\theta_{\text{wind}} - \theta_d)) \times (u_{10} - u_{\text{threshold}})^3 \times \exp\left(-\frac{SM_{0-7\text{cm}}}{SM_{\text{crit}}}\right) \right]$$
This single active term roughly triples dust event probability ($4.4\%$ vs $1.5\%$) and provides a strong, physically grounded predictive signal.
