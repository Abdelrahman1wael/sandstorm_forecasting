Deep Learning Architecture Overview

Physics-Informed Multi-Modal AI Forecasting Backbone

Sand and dust storms (SDS) are highly non-linear, transboundary hydrometeorological disasters. Traditional Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) models suffer from exponential error growth beyond 72 hours. This dedicated AI Architecture Laboratory presents our complete Dual-Line Machine Learning paradigm—coupling planetary-scale foundation models (AI-GAMFS), aerodynamic friction velocity threshold constraints (PINN), and graph advection networks (ST-GNN) operating over multi-source heterogeneous tensors.
